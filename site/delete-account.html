Delete your Calico account

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

In the app

  1. Open Calico and tap the settings icon on Home.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Check the summary of what will be deleted, then confirm.

Want a copy first? Use Settings → Export before deleting.

Without the app

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the Google account you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Calico account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything: your account and Google sign-in link, all books, movies and shows, reading history, ratings, notes, loans, collections, Up next, settings and every cover photo you took. Nothing is kept except in our host's encrypted backups, which expire within 30 days. Loan reminders already scheduled on your phone are removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.
