Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Calico is an Android app for keeping track of your books, library loans, movies and TV shows. This page explains what the app stores, who else sees any of it, and how to delete it. Calico has no ads, does not sell data, and does not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

What Calico stores

Data Why
Your Google account name, email address and profile picture link, received when you sign in with Google To create your account and show who is signed in
Your library: books, movies and shows you add, their status, pages read, ratings, notes, collections and your Up next list This is the app
Loans: the library or friend's name you enter, and borrowed, due and returned dates To show what's due and remind you
Book cover photos you take or choose To show your cover and to read the title and author for you
Settings such as your reading goal, reminder time and theme To remember your choices

This data is stored with Supabase, our database and file host, in a private account that only you can read. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. A copy is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

Services that receive some of it

  • Google Gemini API. When you photograph a cover, the photo is sent from our server to Google's Gemini API, which returns the title, author and other details it can read. Only the photos are sent, not your name or email. Google handles this data under the Gemini API terms.
  • Open Library and Google Books. When you scan a barcode, our server looks up the ISBN. Only the ISBN is sent.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database). When you search for a movie or show, our server sends the search text to TMDB and saves the details it returns. Posters and stills are loaded by your phone directly from image.tmdb.org, so TMDB can see your IP address, as any website would.
  • Sentry. If the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent to Sentry: the error and where in the code it happened, your phone model, Android and app version, the screen you were on, and a random account ID. Crash reports never include your email, book or show titles, search text, notes or photos; the app removes them before anything is sent.
  • Google Sign-In. Google confirms who you are when you sign in.

These providers act on our behalf. We don't share your data with anyone else, and we don't sell it.

What stays on your phone

Loan reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications; no notification service receives your data. Camera and notification permissions are asked only when you use those features, and the app works without them.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Export gives you everything as JSON, or your books as a spreadsheet (CSV).
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, library and photos at once. See Delete your account, including how to ask by email.
  • You can edit or delete any book, movie, show, note or photo in the app at any time.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or delete your account. After an account is deleted, its data can remain in our host's encrypted backups until those expire (at most 30 days), and is then gone.

Children

Calico is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.
