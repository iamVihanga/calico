Refactored end to end on the Calico system, not just re-skinned:

- **Surfaces** — cream paper page with warm ink dot texture, 20px cards on warm-brown shadows, 28px sheet corners, floating pill nav with marmalade active pills.
- **Type** — Playfair display headings, Nunito UI, and Caveat handwriting as a running voice ("from the library", "bring it back!", "let Kiri choose", "wider spine, longer book").
- **Covers are now real objects** — every book/film/show gets a deterministic colour from the `--cover-*` rotation with spine notch, fore-edge radius and cover shadow; they carry the grid, shelf, queue, collections, pick-deck and detail heroes.
- **Focal moments** — forest-green hero panels for Continue reading, the reading-progress card and Next up; marmalade for all progress, primary actions and the episode grid.
- **Loan card** rebuilt as a warm date card with marmalade date stamps, struck-through renewals and a paprika OVERDUE band; overdue also tints the home card's edge, stamp and copy.
- Kept intact: the +30 due-date increment and the IMDb rating pill beside each episode's air status.
